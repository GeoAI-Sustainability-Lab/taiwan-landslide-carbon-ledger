# Data licensing and attribution

The tables in `results/` and the figures in `figures/` are **derived products** computed from openly available sources. No new
licence is placed over the underlying data: the obligations of each source licence follow the derived products, and anyone
reusing them should carry the attributions below forward.

## Sources

Agency of Rural Development and Soil and Water Conservation, Ministry of Agriculture, Taiwan.
https://tech.ardswc.gov.tw/Results/EventBasedLandslideInventory, https://tech.ardswc.gov.tw/Results/TaiwanLandslideInventory
